~ iv · a list and a curfew ~

An agent with a shopping list, and a curfew.

You approve a limit once, like UPI Reserve Pay: ₹12,500 per order, ₹35,000 a month. The agent searches, builds a cart and asks to pay, in chat or voice, in ten Indian languages.

~ v · no sweet-talking ~

Limits live in code, not in the prompt.

Blocked categories, per-order limits and the monthly budget are checked by the server. The budget is reserved atomically before a checkout opens, so two parallel carts can never overspend.

~ vi · two gates ~

Checked before checkout, again before capture.

Orders use manual capture. After the buyer pays in Razorpay Checkout, the server verifies the signature, then checks the payer's UPI ID, card and phone from this payment before any money is captured.

~ vii · stopped at the door ~

The risky one stops at the door.

A second account pays from a UPI ID three ring members used: 0.61, not captured. The customer is told their bank may show a temporary hold that Razorpay reverses automatically, not a vague "nothing was charged".

~ viii · pull the plug ~

A crash can't charge twice.

"Capture requested" is written before Razorpay is called. If the worker dies mid-capture, a reconciler asks Razorpay what happened and records it exactly once.

~ ix · a burglar, invited ~

We hired a burglar. A synthetic one.

Eight playbooks: fraud rings, card testing, account takeover, synthetic identities, device farms, layering cycles, mule fan-out and collusion. It adapts whatever got caught.

~ x · the walls thicken ~

Defences harden, measured on unseen attacks.

A simulator searches 216 policies, prices the cost to good customers, then replays fresh attacks. ₹14,46,140 of unseen attack traffic: rules alone stop 22.4%, the graph 86.7%, the hardened policy 93.9%, with no good orders blocked.

~ xi · climb back in time ~

Go on, drag time around.

Every link and label carries when it became known and where it came from. A replay rebuilds the graph as it was at decision time and re-runs the stored model and policy.

~ xii · no peeking at the future ~

Evidence that arrived later is ignored.

The graph event log, decision records, model files and the hash-chained ledger live in the database. After a restart the replay is still identical, and a missing model is reported as "not replayable", never re-scored.
